## How did you get into pinball?
Through my earlier passion for lighting and laser technology, I worked as a student employee during my studies at Medialas in Balingen, a company that
built show laser projectors. My boss at the time had a Williams Demolition Man (great machine!) in the showroom there, which I then always played after work.


## Why were you out of the hobby for almost 13 years – and what brought you back?
Phew. Back then I had bought my first pinball machine (a Judge Dredd) and restored it too. But my girlfriend at the time wanted to go on holiday, and as a student I had no money,
so the machine had to go and I sold it. And somehow, with various job changes and focusing on my career, it all kind of fizzled out.
The fascination never went away, though. Buying a machine of my own again was just somehow never on the table, and during Covid the prices went through the roof.
And still it got me again, and funnily enough my current girlfriend and fiancée only has herself to blame, because she gave me an advent calendar
where you had to build a pinball machine out of cardboard over 24 days ;) For Christmas I then got myself a Johnny Mnemonic and restored that one as well :)


## How many pinball machines do you have at home?
Currently 2 real ones (a Barrels of Fun "Dune" and a Stern "John Wick" Premium) and my self-built VPIN.
In addition, a Sega "Starship Troopers" and a Williams "Johnny Mnemonic" are at the pinball museum in Eschbach, but they belong to me.
A High Speed is currently being restored as well... Oh dear... That somehow became a lot very quickly....


## Which is your favourite pinball machine?
Dune and Judge Dredd. Judge Dredd certainly because it was the first machine I ever owned, and Dune is simply, just like the film, an absolute audiovisual knockout. I would even
go so far as to say that for me Dune has become the best pinball machine of all time. The theme is implemented perfectly, it's just a huge amount of fun to play and it doesn't get
boring.


## What is the Flippermuseum Eschbach and what do you do there?
The Flippermuseum in Eschbach is a club of other crazy people who are wired the way I am. Strangely enough, I'm one of the youngest there, hehe :D
I'm a member, some of my machines are there, and I simply love tinkering with the machines there, repairing and modding them, and chatting with
like-minded people.


## Real pinball or virtual pinball?
Quite clearly: real pinball. You can do a lot with virtual pinball machines these days, but unfortunately they can't replace the real feeling, including real physics.


## Do you repair or restore pinball machines for other people?
So far nobody has ever asked :D As a rule, though, it's hard to make it work financially for the potential "customer". But I have done things
like the playfield of an Eight Ball Deluxe for the pinball museum. If you have a project or need help, just get in touch.
I'm sure we can talk about a lot of things.


## I want to buy my first pinball machine – what should I look out for?
Oof.... Pinball machines are very expensive. Even the old ones, and there's a lot of cheating and lying going on... As with all expensive toys. I'd recommend
coming by our pinball museum (or a similar place near you) and talking to people who know their stuff and have been in the hobby for a while.
The Flippermarkt forum is certainly always a good starting point for getting a taste of the hobby, too. If you want to buy a new machine and have the necessary
cash, go to a dealer like Freddy's Pinball Paradise, Pinball Universe or RS-Pinball and play as many machines as possible to find out which one
you like.


## Where do you get spare parts for old pinball machines?
There are various specialist shops online such as Pinwitch, PU-Parts, Freddy's Pinball Paradise and so on, or there's the Flippermarkt forum.


## Which 3D printer and CAD software do you use?
I use FreeCAD and have a Bambu Lab P1S.


## Do you share your 3D models and mods?
Yes, when it makes sense I upload them to Thingiverse (e.g. my Stern apron lighting). Otherwise just ask and I'll happily make everything available (and then upload it).

the copters.
